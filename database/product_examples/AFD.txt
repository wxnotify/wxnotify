.SHORT TERM /THROUGH TUESDAY NIGHT/...
Issued at 237 AM MDT Mon Oct 6 2025

Will have clear skies and below normal temperatures continuing
through Tuesday. Highs today slightly warmer than Sunday with
mid to upper 50s valleys and 40s and 50s mountains. Will still
have widespread below freezing lows tonight. Will hold off on
any advisories or warnings to see if this morning results in a
hard freeze or not. Tuesday highs will rise to the 50s and 60s

30s once again. No wind impacts are expected.

.LONG TERM /WEDNESDAY THROUGH MONDAY/...
Issued at 237 AM MDT Mon Oct 6 2025

Wednesday through Friday will see a return to southwesterly flow
aloft and much warmer and above normal temperatures. Highs will
return to the 70s valleys and 60s to lower 70s mountains.
Wednesday and Thursday will be dry with the threat for
precipitation returning Friday. Much colder and showery
conditions are expected Saturday through next Monday with high
temperatures back into the 40s to 50s and the potential for very
high elevation snow showers once again.
