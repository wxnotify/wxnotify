RADIOLOGICAL HAZARD WARNING
COLORADO EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
RELAYED BY NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE DENVER/BOULDER CO
830 AM MDT Tue Apr 22 2025

THE FOLLOWING MESSAGE IS TRANSMITTED AT THE REQUEST OF
THE COLORADO EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY.

A **Radiological Hazard Warning** is in effect for northeastern Boulder County, including the city of Lyons, due to a transportation accident involving radioactive materials on U.S. Highway 36 near Mile Marker 28.

Responders have confirmed that radioactive material has been released in the immediate area. Residents within a **1-mile radius** of the incident should **evacuate immediately**.

Those within **3 miles downwind** (east-southeast) should **shelter indoors**, close all windows and doors, and turn off air conditioning or ventilation systems until further notice.

Follow instructions from local officials. Do not approach the affected area. Updates will follow as monitoring continues.
