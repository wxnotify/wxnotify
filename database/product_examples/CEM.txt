CIVIL EMERGENCY MESSAGE
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT CASS COUNTY ND
RELAYED BY NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE GRAND FORKS ND
845 PM CDT Thu Jul 17 2025

THE FOLLOWING MESSAGE IS TRANSMITTED AT THE REQUEST OF
CASS COUNTY EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT.

This is a **Civil Emergency Message** for residents of the city of Kindred and surrounding rural areas.

Due to a prolonged power outage and contamination of the municipal water supply, **a boil-water advisory is in effect until further notice**. Bottled water is being distributed at the Kindred High School parking lot.

Emergency shelters remain open at the Cass County Community Center for residents needing assistance.

Do not attempt to use tap water for drinking or cooking until authorities declare it safe.
Monitor local media, social media, or NOAA Weather Radio for updates.
