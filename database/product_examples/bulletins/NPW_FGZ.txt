* WHAT...South winds 25 to 35 mph with gusts up to 50 mph expected.

  Navajo, Coconino, Yavapai and northern Gila counties.

* WHEN...From 8 AM MST /9 AM MDT/ to 8 PM MST /9 PM MDT/ Sunday.

* IMPACTS...Gusty winds will blow around unsecured objects. Tree

  along the higher terrain.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Winds this strong can make driving difficult, especially for high
profile vehicles, such as RVs, campers, and tractor trailers. Use
extra caution.

Secure loose outdoor objects that could be blown around or damaged by
the wind.
