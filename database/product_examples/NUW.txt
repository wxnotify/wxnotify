
BULLETIN - EAS ACTIVATION REQUESTED
NUCLEAR POWER PLANT WARNING
PENNSYLVANIA EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY
RELAYED BY NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE MOUNT HOLLY NJ
300 PM EDT Tue May 14 2024

THE FOLLOWING MESSAGE IS TRANSMITTED AT THE REQUEST OF
THE PENNSYLVANIA EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY.

A **Nuclear Power Plant Warning** is in effect for communities within
a **10-mile radius of the Limerick Generating Station** in Montgomery County.
Plant officials have reported an event involving loss of cooling capability
to one reactor unit. A potential release of radioactive material is possible.

Residents in the affected area are advised to **prepare for immediate evacuation**.
Those outside the 10-mile area but within 20 miles should **shelter indoors**,
close windows and doors, and turn off ventilation systems until further notice.

Follow instructions from local officials and stay tuned to radio, television,
or NOAA Weather Radio for updates.
