This message is being transmitted at the request of the United States Geological Survey.
A warning level volcano warning has been issued for Mount Adams. Mount Adams is forecasted to erupt within the next 24 hours. Large amounts of ash will be released. All residents within 50 miles of Mount Adams are under a mandatory evacuation order. Bring only the essentials with you. Volcanic ash from the eruption may contaminate drinking water and will cause trouble breathing. Do not return to the evacuation zone until a code green volcano warning is broadcast. Further evacuations will be issued as needed.
