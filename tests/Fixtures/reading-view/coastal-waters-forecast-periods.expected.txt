.Synopsis For High Island to the Matagorda Ship Channel Out 60 NM including Galveston and Matagorda Bays.

Northern parts of Galveston Bay will see some low water conditions through Friday's low tide cycles. Otherwise, winds and seas are expected to decrease into Friday as surface high pressure moves into the area. Onshore flow resumes late Friday and winds and seas will begin increasing Saturday and Saturday night in advance of the next cold front that will push into the waters on Sunday. Small Craft Advisories will likely be needed both ahead of and behind the front. There is a non-zero chance of some fog a few hours before the front's arrival early Sunday morning, but wind speeds and direction appear fairly unfavorable for dense fog at the current time.
