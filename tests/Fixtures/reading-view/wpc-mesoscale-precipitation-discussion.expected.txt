Areas affected...eastern Iowa into northern/central Illinois

Concerning...Heavy rainfall...Flash flooding possible

Valid 111338Z - 111738Z

Summary...Isolated/spotty flash flood potential exists from the Cedar Rapids area east-southeast into portions of Illinois over the next 2-4 hours (through 17Z/11a central).

Discussion...A small cluster of convection has materialized in east-central Iowa with local training noted near the Cedar Rapids area.  The cells are being maintained by 1) convergent flow in the 850-700mb layer, 2) steep lapse rates (7.5 C/km) from cool air aloft, and 3) a vigorous mid-level shortwave trough moving through the region.  The orientation of the axis of convergence (and resultant convection) is parallel to westerly steering flow aloft, allowing for local training and spots of rain rates exceeding 1.5 inch/hr, which is also surpassing local FFG thresholds.  Flash flood potential exists in the near term.

Over time, models/CAMs and observations hint at persistence of this cluster eastward into northern IL through at least 17Z. Local/limited training of cells will pose a continued flash flood threat as local FFG exceedance (and 1+ inch/hr rain rates) is possible.  This threat should remain isolated though.  Cells should reach the Peoria/Bloomington/Peru region of IL through 16-17Z.
