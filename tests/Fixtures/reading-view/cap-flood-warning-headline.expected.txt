...The National Weather Service in Wilmington has issued a Flood Warning for the following rivers in North Carolina...
Northeast Cape Fear River Near Burgaw affecting Pender County.
* WHAT...Minor flooding is forecast.
* WHERE...Northeast Cape Fear River near Burgaw.
* WHEN...From Friday morning to late Monday night.
* IMPACTS...At 12.0 feet, Flooding worsens in the River Bend, River Birch and Alligator Lake subdivisions. Flooding on Croomsbridge RD and Old Maple Hill RD worsens and the roads may be impassable in multiple locations.  Holly Shelter Estates begins to flood and flooding in Sandy Bend worsens.  Flooding worsens near the ends of Turtle Hill TR, Hickory Hill LN, White Tail LN, Mallard Roost DR, and Oxbow Landing.  Shaw Hwy is flooded at Ashes and Lillington Creeks. Equine Landing RD area may flood.
* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
- At 8:30 AM EST Thursday the stage was 4.0 feet.
- Forecast...The river is expected to rise above flood stage late tomorrow morning to a crest of 11.6 feet early Saturday afternoon. It will then fall below flood stage late Monday morning.
- Flood stage is 10.0 feet.
