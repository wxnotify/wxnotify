Mesoscale Discussion 2465
NWS Storm Prediction Center Norman OK
0330 PM CST Wed Nov 25 2026

Areas affected...Parts of southeastern Missouri and southwestern
Illinois

Concerning...Heavy snow 

Valid 252130Z - 260300Z

SUMMARY...Areas of heavy snow with rates near 1 inch per hour are
possible later this afternoon and evening (around 6pm-9pm CST).

DISCUSSION...Preceding a midlevel low tracking southeastward toward
the Ozarks, regional VWP and mosaic radar data indicate a broad
region of warm-advection-driven precipitation expanding northward
across the Middle MS Valley region. Despite marginal low-level
thermal profiles, cooling associated with increasing precipitation
rates and strengthening ascent should support a gradual transition
to snow across parts of the area over the next few hours. As this
occurs, strengthening low/mid-level frontogenesis in the deformation
zone of the approaching system should focus strengthening mesoscale
ascent through a deeply saturated profile, including within the
dendritic growth zone. This should support areas of heavy snow with
rates near 1 inch per hour -- especially beneath any localized
banding that evolves. 

Current thinking is that the potential for localized banding and
1-inch per hour snowfall rates will occur between 6pm-9pm CST, when
the stronger mesoscale ascent should overlap the cooling thermal
profiles. There is considerable uncertainty on precipitation type
with eastward extent into parts of southern IL, given the deep plume
of warm advection and associated warm nose -- which could favor a
relatively sharp transition from snow to sleet/rain. Moderate to
heavy snow is expected to persist through the overnight hours across
parts of southeastern MO and southwestern IL.
